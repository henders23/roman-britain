## Notes from research
- group a: 1. KIND 'conflict' IS NOT IN dataset.json. The brief lists it, but datasets/early-britain/dataset.json has no 'conflict' kind. Seven of the rows here use it (the river battle, Mona, the three town destructions and the final battle), so the editor must add {"id":"conflict","label":"Battle, raid or revolt"} to dataset.json or those rows will fail validation.
2. Revolt date: every revolt row (Mona, Camulodunum, Londinium, Verulamium, final battle) uses date_start 60 with precision circa, canonical ids end in -60, and each uncertainty note gives the 60/61 debate. Tacitus places the events under the consuls of 61.
3. CIL VI 920 is confirmed; it is also catalogued as CIL VI 31203 and 40416, and as ILS 216. The numeral 'eleven' is partly restored.
4. Not confirmed online: exact Dio section numbers beyond what the LacusCurtius Book 62 page and the secondary pages showed (60.20 is widely cited; 62.1-2 for Camulodunum and 62.1 for London are standard but were not checked). Also Tacitus Agricola 14, and the modern titles marked as such in evidence (Barrett 1991, Frere & Fulford 2001, Hind 1989, Manley 2002, Millett & Wilmott 2003, Wallace 2014, Niblett 2001, Webster 1978, Hingley & Unwin 2005). I am confident of these from standard bibliography but did not search each one. The Richborough primary_locator_2 gives the Bushe-Fox reports as a set and does not name the volume with the ditches.
5. Images: No PD drawing of the Arch of Claudius inscription could be confirmed. The Commons photographs of the fragments are modern and not PD, and I could not tell what 'Arch-of-Claudius.jpg' shows, so camulodunum-surrender-43 has no image. 'Druids Inciting the Britons to Oppose the Landing of the Romans' (Cassell's) exists but may show Caesar's landing, and its artist attribution (Zier, died 1924) is unclear, so I left it out.
