# St Cuthbert's community on the move
By: UK Atlas (atlas synthesis)
Order: chronological

For more than a century the community that guarded St Cuthbert's body moved across northern England, until it settled at Durham. The journey is told by the community's own later histories, which are sometimes more concerned with the saint's authority than with exact dates. Only the stopping places are shown; nothing is drawn between them.

## Stop: lindisfarne-raid-793
The journey's background: the first recorded Viking attack on England fell on Lindisfarne, Cuthbert's monastery. News of it shocked scholars as far away as Charlemagne's court.

## Stop: cuthbert-community-leaves-lindisfarne-875
With Viking armies in the north, the community left the island in 875, carrying Cuthbert's body. The date and the story come from the community's own later histories.

## Stop: cuthbert-community-chester-le-street-883
After some years of wandering, the community settled at Chester-le-Street, where it stayed for more than a century.

## Stop: cuthbert-community-durham-995
In 995 the community moved to Durham, where Cuthbert's shrine still lies. A later story says they found the place by following a woman looking for her cow; the pack keeps that tale off the map.
